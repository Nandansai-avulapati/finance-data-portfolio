Learning where finance and data meet

I am studying Data Science and Finance at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, with an expected graduation in May 2028. Before attending UNC Charlotte, I earned an Associate in Arts from Central Piedmont Community College. My coursework connects financial decision-making with questions about how data is collected, interpreted, and communicated. This portfolio brings those interests together through a project about company finances and stock prices.

My experience includes customer service, nonprofit leadership, and team sports. As a customer associate at UPS, I supported small business customers, processed transactions, and handled competing requests. As president of PGNF, I managed more than $5,000 in fundraising, budgeting, and resource allocation for education and community initiatives. I also served as an Ultimate Frisbee captain and now serve as secretary of my college team. These roles involve communication, organization, and responsibility to other people.

The stock project explores whether positive revenue growth and profit margins are associated with later price increases. It uses API-collected data, pandas cleaning, and two charts. An important lesson from the analysis is that missing information should not automatically become zero. Timing also matters: financial information should precede the outcome being studied. Although the charts show differences between groups, they do not establish causation or dependable investment forecasts. AI assisted with the code and explanations; reviewing those choices and understanding their limits remains part of the learning process.

I am still exploring my career goals rather than committing to a specific role. Studying both finance and data science gives me different areas to investigate through coursework, projects, and practical experience. For now, my next steps are to build a stronger understanding of analytical tools, communicate findings clearly, and learn which kinds of work fit my interests. This portfolio documents that exploration and gives me a starting point for future projects.
